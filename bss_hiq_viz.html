What HIQ* communicates that BSS cannot

Three concrete examples from this dataset
Scale anchoring. LightGBM BSS* = 0.135 — good or bad? HIQ* = 113.2 says it is 0.9 SD above the reference panel mean: clearly above-average.
Patient-mix correction flips a ranking. Regional Hospital looks weakest by naive BSS (0.117) but ranks joint-first by BSS* (0.150) after transport weighting adjusts for its older, lower-acuity mix.
Uncertainty is part of the score. Neural Net HIQ* = 10.1 ± 6.8 — the CI spans −3 to +23. The model is likely harmful (below 85) but the estimate is wide; the GBM CI [107.6, 118.8] is entirely above 100.
